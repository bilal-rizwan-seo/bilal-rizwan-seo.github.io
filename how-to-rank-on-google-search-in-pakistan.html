How to Rank on Google Search in Pakistan

A practical 8-step guide from 6+ years of SEO work, for Pakistani businesses and bloggers.

Fix technical basics

Make sure Google can crawl and index your site: Search Console setup, sitemap, fast mobile pages, HTTPS and no broken links.

Research Pakistani search intent

Pakistanis search in English, Urdu and Roman Urdu. Research all three and match each keyword to the right page type.

Build topical authority

Cover your topic completely with a pillar page and supporting articles, linked together.

Optimize on-page elements

Clear titles, headings, internal links, images with alt text and schema markup.

Win local search

Complete your Google Business Profile, add your city to key pages and collect genuine reviews.

Earn relevant links

Get mentions from Pakistani news sites, directories and industry partners.

Prepare for AI search

Answer questions directly, use clear entities and FAQ sections so AI Overviews can cite you.

Measure and improve

Track clicks, rankings and leads monthly and update what works.
