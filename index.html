FAQ

Questions clients usually ask.

What kind of SEO do you specialize in?

Semantic SEO and topical authority, backed by technical SEO, keyword and intent research, content strategy, link building and optimization for AI search experiences (AEO and GEO).

Which industries have you worked with?

Mostly healthcare and aesthetic clinics, medical billing and virtual medical assistant companies, multi-region eCommerce (Shopify), and local service businesses such as logistics, catering, solar and construction.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. Most of my projects are in the USA, UK, UAE, KSA, Canada and Europe. I work remotely from Islamabad across time zones.

How long before SEO shows results?

Usually 3 to 6 months for meaningful movement, depending on competition, site health and content depth. On Dynamic Clinic, organic leads grew from about 150 to 2,000+ a month over the engagement.

Do you also run Google Ads or email marketing?

Yes. I've managed Google Ads for healthcare (Raha Health) and Klaviyo email marketing for GK Hair, alongside SEO, so paid, owned and organic channels work together.
